Brighton Story Trail: privacy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Brighton Story Trail is a free app made by Stephen Cousins to help you find the BookBench sculptures on the Story Trail in Brighton and Hove. It doesn't collect any personal data.

Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location to show where you are on the trail map and to tell you when you reach a sculpture. Your location is used on your phone only. It is never sent to me or to anyone else, and it isn't stored.

Your progress

The sculptures you've found, your quiz answers and your map settings are saved on your phone so they're still there next time. They never leave your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

No accounts, tracking or adverts

There's no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app doesn't talk to any server.

Directions

If you tap for walking directions, the app opens Google Maps with the destination. What happens there is covered by Google's own privacy policy.
