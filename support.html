Brighton Story Trail: help

Brighton Story Trail helps you find the 32 big BookBench sculptures and 41 small ones around Brighton and Hove, plan a walking route between them and tick them off as you go.

Getting help

If something isn't working, or a bench is in the wrong place on the map, email stephen@filmmyrun.com. It helps to say which bench or screen it's about.

Common questions

The map doesn't show where I am. Check that location is on for Story Trail in Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services.

A route says I haven't finished it. A route counts as done once you've found every bench on it, in any order.
